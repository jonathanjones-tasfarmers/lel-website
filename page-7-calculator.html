Why this calculator exists

This tool is one of two key deliverables required under the project's grant deed: an economic feasibility calculator to help Tasmanian livestock producers assess whether the lick block is a viable option for their own operation using real numbers rather than projections.

The calculator is built on the measured difference between the lick block and control groups in this trial and can be applied to your own herd size, growth rate and cost of production. As the trial continues and more farms come online and contribute data, the numbers behind the project become more reliable. For now, the results should be treated as the current best available evidence, not a final answer.

About this estimate ▾

This estimate uses your own mob numbers plus the lick-block-vs-control difference measured in the trial so far, not a market forecast. It does not account for seasonal price variation, transport, or other on-farm costs. Not financial advice, for a full assessment of your operation, speak with your accountant or farm business advisor alongside using this tool.
