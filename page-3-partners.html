Who's behind the trial

One trial, four partners, a single set of published numbers.

No single organisation runs this on its own, funding, science, supply and on-farm delivery each sit with a different partner. Here's who does what.

Project lead

TasFarmers holds the grant and runs the project, coordinating partners, publishing results, and making sure the findings reach every Tasmanian producer, not just those in the trial.

Tasmanian Government

Funding partner

A $4 million grant funds the trial in full, on the condition that findings, an economic feasibility tool and an adoption handbook are published for the whole sector.

Research partner

The Tasmanian Institute of Agriculture assists in designing the trial protocol, oversees data quality, and independently analyses the results, including where SeaFeed doesn't pay off.

Supplement manufacturer

Sea Forest grows and processes the Asparagopsis seaweed and supplies the SeaFeed lick blocks used across every trial site.

Project timeline

A five-year trial structured around six milestones agreed with the Tasmanian Government, each with its own deliverables and reporting.

✓ Milestone 1 · Completed

19 Jun 2024 – 27 Sep 2024

Foundation Activities

Project plan and budget, communications and engagement strategy, data protocols under the National Farmers Federation Farm Data Code, risk management plan, and NRE Tasmania Animal Ethics Committee approval.

✓ Milestone 2 · Completed

Oct 2024 – 5 May 2025

On-Farm Induction Activities

Feed administration protocols established, animals inducted onto SeaFeed, baseline induction weights recorded, and quarterly stakeholder reporting begins.

✓ Milestone 3 · Completed

June 2026

On-Farm Activities & Reporting, P1

Execution of a formal Deed variation, and submission and acceptance of an updated Project Plan, Risk Management Plan and Agreed Budget, re-profiling the trial to focus primarily on pasture-based beef through to the end of 2028, and introducing a new, separate framework for future sheep and wool trials.

The dairy and feedlot components did not proceed as originally scoped, following the absence of an established Maximum Residue Limit affecting dairy processor participation, and a conservative commercial decision by the feedlot partner.

Milestone 4 · Current phase

Jul 2026 – Aug 2026

On-Farm Activities & Reporting, P2

Continuous grazing trials for beef, with a formal Progress Report due by 30 September 2026.

Milestone 5 · Upcoming

Sep 2026 – 31 Dec 2028

On-Farm Activities & Reporting, P3

Long-term pasture-based beef trials and sheep and wool cohorts run through to completion, with bi-annual Progress and Data Reports tracking productivity, emissions, residues and product quality.

Milestone 6 · Upcoming

Jan 2029 – 1 Feb 2029

Final Report and Publication

Final Report analysing all cumulative data, audited Financial Acquittal submitted, and publication of the producer adoption handbook, economic feasibility calculator and operational viability report.
