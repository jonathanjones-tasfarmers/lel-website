Who's behind the trial

One trial, four partners, one set of published results.

No single organisation delivers this type of project on its own. Funding, scientific oversight, supplement supply and on-farm delivery each sit with a different partner. Here is who does what.

Project lead

TasFarmers holds the grant and runs the project, coordinating partners, publishing results and making sure the findings reach every Tasmanian producer, not just those participating in the trial.

Research partner

The Tasmanian Institute of Agriculture assists with trial protocol design, oversees data quality and independently analyses the results, including where the results do not show a financial benefit.

Supplement manufacturer

Sea Forest is a Tasmanian business supplying the SeaFeed™ technology used across all trial sites.

Tasmanian Government

Funding partner

A $4 million Tasmanian Government grant supports this trial, with the requirement that its findings, economic feasibility tool and adoption handbook are published for the benefit of the entire sector.

The shared brief

Four organisations, one published number

Funding, scientific oversight, supplement supply and on-farm delivery each sit with a different partner, but the ambition is shared: to publish the real-time results, good or bad, so every Tasmanian producer has the information needed to make an educated decision.

The $4 million Tasmanian Government grant that funds this work comes with that condition attached. In addition, an economic feasibility tool and an adoption handbook are contractual deliverables, not optional extras.

Project timeline

A five-year trial structured around six milestones agreed with the Tasmanian Government, each with defined deliverables and reporting requirements.

✓ Milestone 1 · Completed

19 Jun 2024 – 27 Sep 2024

Foundation Activities

Project plan and budget, communications and engagement strategy, data protocols aligned with the National Farmers Federation Farm Data Code, risk management plan, and approval from the NRE Tasmania Animal Ethics Committee.

✓ Milestone 2 · Completed

Oct 2024 – 5 May 2025

On-Farm Induction Activities

Feed administration protocols were established, animals were inducted onto SeaFeed™, baseline induction weights were recorded, and quarterly stakeholder reporting begins.

✓ Milestone 3 · Completed

June 2026

On-Farm Activities & Reporting, P1

Execution of a formal Deed variation, and submission and acceptance of an updated Project Plan, Risk Management Plan and Agreed Budget, re-profiling the trial to focus primarily on pasture-based beef through to the end of 2028, and introducing a new, separate framework for future sheep and wool trials.

The dairy and feedlot components did not proceed as originally scoped, following the absence of an established Maximum Residue Limit affecting dairy processor participation, and a conservative commercial decision by the feedlot partner.

Milestone 4 · Current phase

Jul 2026 – Aug 2026

On-Farm Activities & Reporting, P2

Continuous grazing trials for beef, with a formal progress report due by 30 September 2026.

Milestone 5 · Upcoming

Sep 2026 – 31 Dec 2028

On-Farm Activities & Reporting, P3

Long-term pasture-based beef trials and sheep and wool cohorts run through to completion. Bi-annual progress and data reports will track productivity, emissions, residues and product quality.

Milestone 6 · Upcoming

Jan 2029 – 1 Feb 2029

Final Report and Publication

A final report will analyse the complete dataset, accompanied by an audited financial acquittal. The producer adoption handbook, economic feasibility calculator and operational viability report will also be published.
