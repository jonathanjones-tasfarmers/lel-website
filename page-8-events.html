Ways to get involved

What to expect

Three formats, so you can join in whichever suits your season and your questions.

On-farm field days

Walk the paddock with the host farmer and project team, see the OptiWeigh units in action and talk through what the trial is finding.

Workshops

Hands-on sessions on the economics, feeding and management of low emissions feed supplements, using your own herd numbers.

Webinars

Short online updates on results to date, with time for questions. Join from the farm or the office; recordings shared afterwards.

Visiting a farm? Field days are held on working farms. Please arrive with clean boots and a clean vehicle, follow the host's biosecurity and safety instructions on the day, and let us know about any access needs when you register. Venue details and parking are sent with your registration.
