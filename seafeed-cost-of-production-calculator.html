This treats your current weight as already paid for, only the cost and revenue of the extra kilos gained during this period are counted, not the animal's starting value. That's why margin stays positive even if your cost of production matches your sale price exactly. It's gross margin over the finishing period, not full farm profit.

About this estimate ▾

This estimate uses your own mob numbers plus the SeaFeed-vs-control difference measured in the trial so far, not a market forecast. It does not account for seasonal price variation, transport, or other on-farm costs. Not financial advice, for a full assessment of your operation, speak with your accountant or farm business advisor alongside using this tool.
