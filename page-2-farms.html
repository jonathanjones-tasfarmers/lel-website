Before you weigh it up

Real results from working Tasmanian farms

This is not a prior study extrapolated to your paddock. It involves ordinary Tasmanian pasture-based cattle and sheep, with KleenGrow blocks being incorporated into their normal feeding program, on working commercial properties.

Every result, whether it supports the use of KleenGrow blocks or not, is published as the trial progresses. As a producer considering the use of this supplement for your own operation, you will be seeing the same data that is available to your neighbours.

Why producers are getting involved

Reasons Tasmanian livestock operations are putting their hand up, not in any particular order.

01

Get ahead of the emissions questions

Processors and export markets are asking more on-farm emissions questions every year. Producers participating in this trial will have real numbers to answer with well before it becomes mandatory, helping them prepare for future requirements and supporting supply chain access.

02

Real Tasmanian numbers, not trial data

Most published methane emissions reduction research comes from feedlot trials. This trial is different: Tasmanian pasture, Tasmanian livestock and real Tasmanian production costs.

03

Independent science, not a sales pitch

Delivered in partnership with the Tasmanian Institute of Agriculture and backed by Tasmanian Government funding, the trial reports its findings transparently, including where the results do not show a financial benefit.

04

Tools you can use, whether you joined or not

The grant requires this project to publish an economic feasibility tool and an adoption handbook for every Tasmanian producer, both formal Milestone 6 deliverables due at project completion in February 2029. Work on both is already underway. In the meantime, visit the Data page to explore an illustrative estimate of the carbon value for your own herd.

What using KleenGrow actually involves

The practical realities before considering KleenGrow for your own operation.

How it's fed

Delivered as a lick block

KleenGrow blocks are provided as a free-choice supplement alongside your existing feeding regime, no change to your existing grazing system is required.

Storage and handling

No special infrastructure needed

The blocks store like any other mineral or molasses block on farm and there is no refrigeration, mixing equipment or dedicated infrastructure to budget for.

System fit

Pasture-based, beef and sheep

The trial runs across pasture-based livestock enterprises, not only intensive feeding systems, so the results are relevant whether your animals are grazing or yarded.

Animal health & food safety

Tracked as part of the trial

Animal health, feed intake and any additional requirements are monitored and reported alongside the productivity outcomes, including when the findings are unfavourable.
