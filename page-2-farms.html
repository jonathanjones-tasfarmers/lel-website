Why producers are getting involved

Reasons Tasmanian livestock operations are putting their hand up, not in any particular order.

01

Get ahead of the emissions questions

Processors and export markets are asking more on-farm emissions questions every year. Producers in this trial have real numbers to answer with, well before it's mandatory.

02

Real Tasmanian numbers, not trial data

Most published Asparagopsis research comes from feedlot trials. This is Tasmanian pasture, Tasmanian livestock, and Tasmanian costs.

03

Independent science, not a sales pitch

Delivered with the Tasmanian Institute of Agriculture and backed by Tasmanian Government funding, the trial reports what it finds, including where SeaFeed doesn't pay off.

04

Tools you can use, whether you joined or not

The grant requires this project to publish an economic feasibility tool and an adoption handbook for every Tasmanian producer, both formal Milestone 6 deliverables due at project completion in February 2029. Work on both is already underway. In the meantime, see an illustrative carbon value estimate for your own herd on our Data page.

What using SeaFeed actually involves

The practical realities before you weigh it up for your own operation.

How it's fed

Delivered as a lick block

SeaFeed is fed free-choice as a supplementary lick block alongside your existing feeding regime, no change to your base ration or grazing system required.

Storage & handling

No special infrastructure needed

Lick blocks store like any other mineral or molasses block on farm, no refrigeration, mixing equipment, or dedicated infrastructure to budget for.

System fit

Pasture-based, beef and sheep

The trial runs across paddock-based livestock enterprises, not just intensive feeding systems, the results are relevant whether your animals are grazing or yarded.

Animal health & food safety

Tracked as part of the trial

Animal health, intake and any withholding requirements are monitored and reported alongside the productivity numbers, not left out if the news isn't positive.
